# zodify logo

Refined z uses a custom lowercase z with rounded terminals, flat violet, and no underline. The mark and wordmark are self-contained vector geometry with no font dependency.

- `lockup-light.svg`: violet mark with a dark wordmark on light backgrounds.
- `lockup-dark.svg`: lavender mark with a white wordmark on dark backgrounds.
- `mark-violet.svg` and `mark-lavender.svg`: standalone symbols.

Keep the proportions and clear space. Use the favicon assets at small sizes rather than shrinking the complete wordmark. Palette: violet `#7C3AED`, lavender `#C4A5FF`, dark ink `#241B35`, near-black background `#030014`, white `#FFFFFF`.
